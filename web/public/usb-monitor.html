mBot USB Serial Monitor

This page connects directly to mLink on this laptop. It reads USB serial bytes at 115200 baud. It does not send robot commands, flash firmware, or request a reset.

Close other serial monitors and do not upload while listening. Opening the USB port may itself affect the board's reset lines. Blank output means no bytes received—not a successful boot.
